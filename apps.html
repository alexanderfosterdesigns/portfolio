Highlight to fix
App three / writing

BOOKLI

A lightweight, intuitive alternative to Word built to help creatives write, refine, and publish with less friction. Highlight text, send it into Claude or ChatGPT for repair, surface likely issues, and auto-format manuscripts from the same workspace.

  • Highlight-to-fix workflowEditing starts from the sentence you are touching instead of a side panel maze.
  • AI issue suggestionsMoves beyond spellcheck into structure, tone, and clarity.
  • Publishing formattingKeeps the product aimed at writers who want to finish and ship work.

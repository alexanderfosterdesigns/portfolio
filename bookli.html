BOOKLI

BOOKLI is a lightweight, modern alternative to Word built for writers who want assistance without losing flow. Highlight a passage, send it to Claude or ChatGPT for repair, surface likely issues, and auto-format the manuscript for publishing without bouncing across tools.

Product idea

BOOKLI is designed for writers who want a cleaner, lighter, more intuitive editing space than Word without sacrificing the ability to finish a manuscript properly.

System

The core loop is direct: highlight, ask for help, review the suggestion, and keep moving. Claude and ChatGPT become embedded repair tools instead of separate destinations.

Why it matters here

BOOKLI connects the app work back to the books. It proves the portfolio is not just about writing finished titles, but also about designing the tools that shape them.

Positioning line
A writing workspace built to help creatives move faster, stay in flow, and turn rough text into publishable work.
Core moves
Highlight-to-fix interaction

Issue suggestions

The app aims at clarity, structure, and voice instead of just surface-level corrections.

Publishing output

Product idea

CHERIFLIX treats movie and show discovery as an atmosphere problem as much as a data problem. The interface is there to build anticipation, not just list content.

System

Streaming APIs plus TMDB create a more complete content layer: metadata, discovery rails, and a catalogue that feels grounded in real-world media structure.

Why it matters here

It shows how the portfolio handles entertainment products differently from books or writing tools. The mood shifts, but the level of intention stays high.
